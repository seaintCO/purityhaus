Meet Susan.

Stylist. Mentor. Businesswoman. A believer in beauty that begins with care, confidence and understanding the person in front of her.

Her Approach

Hair is personal. The experience should be too.

Susan's work at Purity Haus is built around listening first. Every client brings a different texture, routine, history and goal — so the appointment should never feel one-size-fits-all.

Her focus is an elevated but welcoming experience: thoughtful conversation, healthy-hair minded care and a finished look that supports the confidence you already have.

Beauty, confidence and community are not separate ideas here. They are the experience.
